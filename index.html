Things I've shipped

Real bottlenecks I got nerd-sniped by, then built with AI pair-programming, way too many browser tabs, and stubbornness.

PROJECT QUEUE+

AI-assisted electronic queue management
LIVE · ADOPTED

A low-cost electronic queue management system for PRC Region III — client queueing, staff processing, a live display board, and voice announcements. Originally built on Google Apps Script/Sheets, migrated to Supabase (Postgres) for real-time updates.

  • Piloted July 2026, later formally adopted via office memorandum
  • Real-time sync across public ticketing, staff counters, and display board
  • Technical report & research manuscript in development
Supabase Postgres Netlify JavaScript

PRVS

Professional Records Verification System
IN USE

A verification portal connecting PRC and law enforcement (PNP ACG) — officers submit credential verification requests per individual, upload required documents, and track status in real time, replacing manual, paper-based coordination.

  • Per-request tracking with status badges and 30-second auto-refresh
  • Built-in "Ask PRC" messaging tied to each request
  • Certificate issuance tracked by issuing office

About

Hi, I'm Jerreld — I build stuff because it's fun

No CS degree, no dev job title. I'm a government employee who fell into building software once AI coding tools made it possible to go from "this workflow is broken" to a working prototype over a weekend. Most of what's on this page started as me being annoyed at something slow and clunky, then refusing to let it go until it worked.

I lean hard on AI pair-programming — Claude, mostly — to move fast and learn as I go, and I'm not shy about it: that's just how I build now. I don't always know the "proper" way to do things, but I ship, I break things, I fix them, and I put the code on GitHub.
