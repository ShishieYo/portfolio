PROJECT QUEUE+

AI-assisted electronic queue management
LIVE · ADOPTED

A low-cost electronic queue management system for PRC Region III — client queueing, staff processing, a live display board, and voice announcements. Originally built on Google Apps Script/Sheets, migrated to Firebase (Firestore) for real-time updates.

  • Piloted July 2026, later formally adopted via office memorandum
  • Real-time sync across public ticketing, staff counters, and display board
  • Technical report & research manuscript in development

PRVS

Professional Records Verification System
IN USE

A verification portal connecting PRC and law enforcement (PNP ACG) — officers submit credential verification requests per individual, upload required documents, and track status in real time, replacing manual, paper-based coordination.

  • Per-request tracking with status badges and 30-second auto-refresh
  • Built-in "Ask PRC" messaging tied to each request
  • Certificate issuance tracked by issuing office
Apps Script Google Sheets HTML/CSS/JS

About

Hello — I'm Jerreld

I'm a government employee and self-directed builder. Outside my role in licensure and registration at PRC Region III, I design and ship the systems I wish existed at work — queueing, verification, and workflow automation — using AI-assisted development, Python, R, and modern web tooling.

Most of what I build starts as a personal initiative to fix a real bottleneck, gets piloted quietly, and — when it proves itself — gets formally adopted. Queue+ went that route: pilot in July 2026, then adopted office-wide by memorandum.
